## Halls and amenities

Culture.RF describes two halls, with capacities of **179 and 124**, a cinema bar selling snacks/drinks/popcorn, and a VIP area with more comfortable transforming seats [S7]. The page does not establish the last date each detail was updated.

The live booking widget for the sampled family-film session explicitly displayed **Зал Малый**, **2D**, an age rating, a seating diagram, and a **270 ₽** price category [S4, S6]. That supports the existence of a small hall and this session's displayed format/price, not a cinema-wide price list or a current audited capacity.

Do not advertise these without fresh confirmation:

- Current hall names, total physical seats, sellable seats, VIP seat categories, and wheelchair positions.
- Projector/sound brands, Dolby/Atmos claims, 3D capabilities, screen dimensions, or equipment quality.
- Entrance accessibility, lifts/ramps, accessible toilets, hearing assistance, sensory screenings, or assistance policies.
- Parking, cloakroom, Wi-Fi, food menus, prices, and whether outside food is permitted.
- Rental packages, private screenings, group booking, birthday parties, corporate events, or school offers.

Culture.RF explicitly reports that facility-accessibility information is absent [S7]. **Absence of information is not evidence of an inaccessible venue**, and neither is it permission to claim accessibility.

## Product context: hypotheses, not market findings

A two-hall city-centre cinema plausibly benefits from fast “what can I watch tonight?” discovery, family-friendly browsing, useful arrival information, and reliable post-purchase support. These are design hypotheses grounded in the observed product, not conclusions from customer interviews or analytics.

Potential audiences: nearby residents, city-centre visitors, families, students/Pushkin Card users, and returning customers who already know the cinema. Their relative sizes, motivations, budgets, and conversion rates are unknown.

Potential business outcomes to measure later: time to select a session, qualified widget opens, completed purchases reported by the authorized provider, support completion, and mobile task success. Do not fabricate conversion improvements or attendance statistics.
